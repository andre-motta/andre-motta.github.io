# Andre Lustosa

I'm a **Principal Software Engineer** at Red Hat, serving as **Team Lead** for the Ecosystems Team within AIPCC (AI Platform Core Components). As a technical leader, I focus on multiplying team impact through architecture, mentorship, and driving strategic technical initiatives across the AI platform infrastructure.

- AWS Neuron
